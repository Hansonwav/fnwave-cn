Installation, licensing, and order support

System, installation, license, and order guidance live here. ReaHanson 0.1.7 for Windows is available to download.

Download for Windows · 0.1.7
01

System requirements

Provisional minimum baseline: Windows 10 22H2 64-bit, an x86-64 four-core CPU, 8 GB RAM, an SSD with at least 5 GB free during installation, and a 1366×768 display at 100% scaling. No dedicated GPU is required.

02

Install and update

Download the 0.1.7 Windows installer, then run it. Try without payment, or activate in the app with your license key.

03

License and devices

The same license may be installed on multiple computers you own, but ReaHanson may be used on only one computer at a time. License details are sent to the order email.

04

Payment and orders

Payment is closed now. At release, Paddle will handle payment, tax, and order receipts.

Support information

Common questions

Will my audio files be uploaded?＋

Your core library and index run locally. Only online AI features you explicitly enable call configured third-party services.

Where is the installer stored?＋

The installer is hosted in Cloudflare R2 and publicly served from downloads.getfnwave.com. The website and license emails share the same versioned link.

What order help does Paddle provide?＋

Paddle handles checkout, payment, applicable taxes, and receipts. ReaHanson support handles product use and licensing.

How do I contact support?＋

For installation, license, or order questions, contact fnwavesupport@gmail.com.
